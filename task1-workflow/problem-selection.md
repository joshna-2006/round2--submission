# 1. Choosing the Process

## Where the candidates came from
On 2026-10-23 a faculty member listed areas of college administration for me: budget and vendor purchasing (price, service, configuration/quantity), equipment, department activities, the approval hierarchy (Governing Body at the Panjagutta head office as financial authority, VC, Director, Deans, HODs), salary, **ID cards**, admin work and accounts work (evidence log E1). I also considered two processes I had looked at earlier.

## Comparison
I scored each candidate 1 (poor) to 3 (good) on the criteria the task needs.

| Candidate | Can I get legitimate evidence in 1–2 days? | Avoids private data? | Clear failure cases? | Time cost measurable? | Can I prototype it with free tools? | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Student ID card replacement** (lost or damaged card) | 3: students who replaced a card can be asked; the ID desk can be asked about the steps | 3: only counts and times are needed | 3: missing photo, duplicate request, someone else collecting, printer failure | 3: visits and days per request | 3 | **15** |
| Department equipment purchase approval | 1: needs the Accounts office; purchase files contain financial data | 1: vendor prices, budgets | 3 | 2 | 3 | 10 |
| Admission document verification | 1: happens once a year; this year's intake is over | 2: certificates are personal documents | 3 | 2: only my own memory from 2023 | 3 | 11 |
| Salary queries | 1 | 1: salary is private | 2 | 1 | 2 | 7 |
| Bonafide certificate request | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 12 |

## Decision
**ID card replacement.** It scores highest mainly because **I can actually check it myself**: I can talk to students who replaced a card and ask the ID desk about the steps, without seeing anyone's personal records. The purchase process is more "important", but I couldn't get honest evidence for it before the deadline, and its evidence (budgets, vendor prices) is the kind I'm not allowed to commit.

[ ] I have reviewed these scores and confirm this choice.

**What this choice gives up:** ID card replacement is a small process. Its total time saving will be modest, and I'll say so rather than inflate it.
