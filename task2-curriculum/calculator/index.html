How the numbers are calculated

Affected credits = credits of every UPDATE course + credits moved from REDUCE to ADD, counted once (max of removed and added).
Affected % = affected ÷ current total × 100. Balanced = proposed total equals current total.
The same calculation is in check_credits.py, which also checks that this page's data equals proposed-curriculum.csv.
