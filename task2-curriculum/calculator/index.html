How the numbers are calculated

Affected credits = (sum of current credits of every UPDATE course) + (credits reallocated from REDUCE to ADD, counted once).
Reallocated credits = max(total credits removed by REDUCE, total credits created by ADD). Counting it once stops the same credit being counted as both "removed" and "added".
Affected % = affected credits ÷ current total credits × 100.
Balanced means proposed total = current total.
Credits supporting selected role = sum of proposed credits of courses tagged with that role.
